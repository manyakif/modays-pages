Wrapping Up the Day

Answer a few questions, and the day is done.

Every evening it asks once: how was today? On nights you've already wrapped up, it doesn't call.

  1. Just answer what it asks

    It goes in order and skips what doesn't apply.

  2. One color is enough

    On a day you wrote nothing, one color is a complete day.

  3. Today's face, at the end

    The day's card appears at the end. See how today will stay, then close.

Closing isn't final. Change your mind later and tap that date to go back in. A few days ago or years ago, it works the same.

Keeping Things Secret

Nothing shows that anything is hidden.

A locked folder is already a confession.

There's nothing more to show you.
Your entries would say the same.

Not locked. Just not there.

Private entries don't get a padlock. They're simply not there.

When someone asks to look

Give them a different passcode. The app opens perfectly with it. Only what you keep private is missing.

When there's no time to type

One gesture you set switches over on the spot.

Off means it doesn't exist

If you don't use it, the word “hidden” appears nowhere in the app.

Archive

Not deleted. Just set aside.

Not wanting to see something and wanting it gone are different things.

It just steps aside

Put an entry in the archive and it leaves the timeline, search, and Photo Pick. One more answer for the things you almost deleted.

Bring it back anytime

Take it out and it returns exactly where it was.

Not the same as hiding

It isn't for keeping things from others. It's a place for what you don't want in view right now.
