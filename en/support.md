moDays is a journaling app that keeps each day in a color, a sentence, and photos.
If you have questions or run into a problem, please check below.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Weather is not showing
In iOS Settings > Apps > Days, make sure Location access is set to "While Using the App".
Location is used to fetch the weather of the moment and to note where you were as a place.
Please also check your internet connection.

### I can't attach photos
Check the Photos permission in iOS Settings > Apps > Days.
If it is set to "Selected Photos", you can add more photos on the same screen.

### Recording doesn't work
Make sure the Microphone permission is enabled in iOS Settings > Apps > Days.

### Calendar events are not showing
Check the Calendars permission in iOS Settings > Apps > Days.
Calendar events are read only to show that day's schedule while you wrap up your day.

### Notifications don't arrive
Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings > Notifications > Days.

### The lock won't open
The lock opens with the passcode you set in the app, or with Face ID (Touch ID on devices that have it).
If Face ID doesn't appear, check the Face ID permission in iOS Settings > Apps > Days.

### iCloud sync isn't working
Make sure you are signed in at iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud, and that the Days toggle is on.
Sync runs automatically over Wi-Fi or cellular.

### How do backups work
In Settings > Backup, you can create backups yourself and restore from the list.
Backups are stored in your own iCloud.

### Does it work with mo.Today
If you use mo.Today with the same Apple account, your completed tasks and routines appear in your daily record.
iCloud must be enabled for both apps in iOS Settings.

### I want to change the app language
Go to Settings > Display > App Language to jump to iOS Settings and change it.

## Tips

- Pull the week bar down to open the calendar; push it up to open the archive.
- You can always go back to a past day and add to it. Finishing later is fine.
